Contact Us

We would love to hear from you, whether you found the perfect name, spotted a bug, or have an idea for the generator.

Email: hello@nameforge.live
We aim to reply within two working days.

What to contact us about

  • Feedback and suggestions: new industries, word ideas, naming styles or features you would like to see.
  • Corrections: if a guide is out of date (fees and rules do change), tell us and we will fix it.
  • Privacy requests: questions about your data or requests to exercise your rights under GDPR. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Advertising and partnerships: we are happy to hear from relevant businesses, such as registrars, accountants or designers serving small businesses.

What we cannot help with

We cannot tell you whether a specific name is legally available or give legal advice about trademarks or company law. For that, please contact the Companies Registration Office, Companies House, the Intellectual Property Office of Ireland or the UK Intellectual Property Office, or speak to a solicitor or trade mark attorney.

The generator itself does not collect any information you type into it, so we cannot recover lost names. Saved favourites live only in your own browser.
