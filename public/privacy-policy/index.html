Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

This Privacy Policy explains how NameForge ("we", "us", "our") collects and uses information when you visit https://nameforge.live (the "Site"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and to complying with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988-2018, the ePrivacy Regulations (S.I. 336 of 2011) and, where applicable, the UK GDPR.

1. Who we are

The Site is operated by Donna Reilly Websites, Ireland, which is the data controller for the purposes of this policy. You can contact us about privacy at hello@nameforge.live.

2. The short version

  • The name generator runs in your browser. We do not receive or store the keywords you type or the names you generate.
  • Your saved favourites are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device. We cannot see them.
  • We do not require accounts, sign-ups or payments.
  • We show advertising from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, but only in line with the choices you make in the consent message shown to visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information processed automatically

Like almost all websites, our hosting provider automatically processes technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request, the date and time, and the referring page. This is used to deliver the Site, keep it secure and diagnose problems. Server logs are kept for a limited period (typically no more than 30 days) unless needed to investigate a security incident.

3.2 Information you give us

If you email us, we receive your email address, name (if given) and the content of your message. We use this only to reply and keep a record of the correspondence.

3.3 Information stored on your device

When you save a name to your shortlist, it is stored in your browser's local storage under the key namegen:favourites. This is strictly necessary to provide a feature you have asked for; it never leaves your device and you can delete it at any time with the "Clear list" button or by clearing your browser data.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense, an advertising service provided by Google Ireland Limited (Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) and Google LLC, to display ads on the Site. Advertising allows us to keep the Site free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie, now part of Google Ad Manager and Google Marketing Platform) enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Other third-party vendors and ad networks certified by Google may also serve ads on the Site and may use cookies, web beacons and similar technologies. You can view the list of these vendors in the consent message and in the "Privacy and cookie settings" link on the Site.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's My Ad Center (formerly Ads Settings). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.youronlinechoices.eu (Europe) or www.aboutads.info (US).
  • For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and How Google uses cookies in advertising.

Consent

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message from a Google-certified consent management platform (CMP) that supports the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. Non-essential advertising cookies are only set, and personalised ads only shown, if you consent. If you do not consent, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads, which use cookies or local storage only for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention, where permitted. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time using the "Privacy and cookie settings" link that appears on the Site.

5. Legal bases for processing

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)): for non-essential cookies and personalised advertising.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): for operating, securing and improving the Site, and for replying to messages you send us.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): where we must keep or disclose information by law.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only with service providers who help us run the Site (our web host and email provider) and with Google and its advertising partners as described above. Some of these providers may process data outside the EEA; where they do, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. External links

The generator includes links to third-party sites such as domain registrars and social networks so you can check whether a name is available. When you click one, the name you are checking is included in the link and the third party's own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for their practices.

8. How long we keep data

Emails are kept for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and for up to two years afterwards, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Server logs are kept as described in section 3.1. Advertising cookies are kept for the periods set by Google and its partners (see our Cookie Policy).

9. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data; to restrict or object to processing; to data portability; and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@nameforge.live. We will respond within one month.

You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission in Ireland (www.dataprotection.ie) or, in the UK, to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

10. Children

The Site is intended for adults and business owners. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add new features or advertising partners. The "last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
