Name suggestions are not checked for availability

The NameForge business name generator creates names automatically from word lists and the keywords you enter. It does not search company registers, business name registers, trademark databases, domain registries or social networks. A name appearing on this Site does not mean it is available, unregistered or free to use. Before using any name, you should at a minimum:

  • search the Companies Registration Office (Ireland) and/or Companies House (UK);
  • search the Intellectual Property Office of Ireland, the UK Intellectual Property Office and the EUIPO (e.g. via TMview);
  • check domain names and social media handles; and
  • search the web for businesses already using the name.
