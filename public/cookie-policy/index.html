Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

This Cookie Policy explains how NameForge uses cookies and similar technologies such as local storage. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and web beacons. Some are strictly necessary for a site to work; others are used for things like measuring visits or showing relevant ads. Under Irish and EU law, non-essential cookies require your consent.

Your choices

  • Consent message: visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland see a consent message when they first visit. You can accept, reject or choose specific purposes and vendors.
  • Change your mind: use the "Privacy and cookie settings" link shown on the Site to review or withdraw consent at any time.
  • Google ad personalisation: manage it at My Ad Center.
  • Industry opt-outs: www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Browser settings: you can block or delete cookies in your browser. Blocking all cookies may stop some features working, but the name generator itself will still work.
