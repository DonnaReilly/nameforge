About NameForge

NameForge is a free business name generator and a small library of practical naming guides, built for sole traders, makers, tradespeople and small business owners.

Why we built it

We work with small businesses in Ireland every day, and naming comes up constantly. Plumbers, bakers, Etsy sellers and new consultants all hit the same wall: they know what they do, but finding a name that sounds right, is not already taken and works as a domain can take weeks. Most name generators we tried were either stuffed with random nonsense words, US-focused, or demanded an email address before showing a single idea.

So we built the tool we wanted: fast, free, no sign-up, with names that actually sound like real businesses, including local and Irish-language options, and one-click links to check the domain and social handles.

How the generator works

The generator runs entirely in your web browser. It combines the words you type with hand-curated word banks for different industries and applies naming techniques used by branding professionals: compounds, blends, alliteration, rhyme, suffixes, invented "brandable" words and local or Irish-language names. A set of quality rules filters out names that are hard to pronounce, too long or unfortunate. Because it runs on your device, what you type is never sent to us.

The names are suggestions. We do not check them against company registers, trademark databases or domain registries, so always do your own checks before using a name. Our trademark guide explains how.

Our guides

Our naming guides are written for real small businesses, with a focus on Ireland and the UK: how the CRO and Companies House work, which trademark registers to search, how to choose between a .ie and a .com, and how to test a name with customers before you commit. We update them when rules or fees change. If you spot something out of date, please let us know.

Who runs NameForge

NameForge is run by Donna Reilly Websites, a small web design business based in Ireland. The site is free to use and is supported by advertising, which is how we keep it free without asking for sign-ups or payments.
